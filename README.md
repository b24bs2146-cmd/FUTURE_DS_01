# FUTURE_DS_01

## Business Sales Performance Analytics

**Internship:** Future Interns — Data Science & Analytics  
**Task:** Task 1 — Business Sales Performance Analytics  
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Power BI

### Project Overview
This project analyzes Superstore sales data to identify business performance trends, profitable product categories, regional performance, and the relationship between discounts and profitability.

### Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify monthly sales trends.
- Compare sales and profit across product categories and regions.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Examine profitability across discount levels.

### Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas and NumPy
- Matplotlib and Seaborn
- Google Colab
- Microsoft Power BI

### Project Files
- `Superstore_Sales_Performance_Analysis.ipynb` — Python data analysis and visualizations.
- `Superstore_Sales_Performance_Dashboard.pbix` — Interactive Power BI dashboard.

### Key Findings
- Total sales were approximately **$2.30 million**.
- Total profit was approximately **$286.40 thousand**.
- Technology generated the highest total profit among the three product categories.
- The West region recorded the highest sales and profit.

The Tables sub-category recorded an overall loss.

Higher discount groups showed negative total profits in this dataset; this is an observed association and does not by itself prove causation.

Conclusion

The analysis highlights sales trends, regional and category performance, and areas where profitability may need attention. The interactive Power BI dashboard supports further exploration of business performance.
